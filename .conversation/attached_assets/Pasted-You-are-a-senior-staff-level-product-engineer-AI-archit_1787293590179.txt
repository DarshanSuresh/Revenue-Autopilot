You are a senior staff-level product engineer, AI architect, fintech engineer, UX designer, and hackathon-winning technical lead.

Build a production-quality hackathon application called:

REVENUE AUTOPILOT
"An autonomous AI revenue-recovery decision engine that finds money at risk, understands WHY revenue is being lost, chooses the optimal intervention, executes bounded recovery actions, and measures incremental revenue recovered."

IMPORTANT:
This is NOT a generic dashboard.
This is NOT a chatbot.
This is NOT a simple failed-payment reminder system.

The core product must demonstrate:
DETECT → DIAGNOSE → PREDICT → DECIDE → ACT → VERIFY → LEARN

The application should feel like a real fintech product that could be deployed by a payment platform or merchant.

==================================================
1. CORE PROBLEM
==================================================

Merchants lose revenue because of:

- payment failures
- checkout abandonment
- subscription failures
- repeated failed retries
- payment-method degradation
- overdue invoices
- customer-specific payment behavior
- poor retry timing
- unnecessary discounts
- customers who should be escalated to humans
- customers who should NOT be contacted again

Most systems simply detect a failure and send a reminder.

Revenue Autopilot must instead answer:

1. How much revenue is at risk?
2. WHY is it at risk?
3. Which customers are actually recoverable?
4. What is the probability of recovery?
5. What intervention should happen?
6. When should it happen?
7. What is the expected financial return?
8. When should the system STOP?
9. How much money was actually recovered?
10. How much incremental revenue did the AI generate compared with baseline strategies?

==================================================
2. CORE AI DECISION FORMULA
==================================================

For every at-risk transaction/customer, calculate:

Expected Recovery Value
-
Intervention Cost
-
Customer Friction Cost
-
Discount Cost
=
Expected Net Recovery Value

The AI should select an action only when the expected value is positive.

Possible actions:

- RETRY_NOW
- RETRY_LATER
- CHANGE_RETRY_TIMING
- SWITCH_PAYMENT_METHOD
- SEND_EMAIL
- SEND_WHATSAPP
- OFFER_LIMITED_INCENTIVE
- ESCALATE_TO_HUMAN
- DO_NOTHING
- STOP_RETRYING

Every AI decision MUST have an explanation.

Example:

Customer:
₹4,999 failed payment

AI decision:
RETRY_LATER

Reason:
- Historical successful retry probability: 72%
- Customer usually completes payments between 7 PM–10 PM
- Previous retry occurred at 10 AM
- Expected recovery: ₹3,599
- Intervention cost: ₹4
- Expected net recovery: ₹3,595
